# Privacy Policy — VoltMaster Pro

Last updated: 2026-07-10

VoltMaster Pro respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data Collection

VoltMaster Pro does not collect, sell, transmit, or share personal data.

## Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

- bookmarks
- saved calculations
- quiz results
- reading progress
- language preference
- recent searches

This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to any server by the app.

## Internet Access

The app may use internet access for:

- opening YouTube tutorial links in an external app/browser
- opening email feedback/support using your email app
- loading online resources if enabled by platform dependencies

The tutorial list itself works offline, but watching YouTube videos requires internet.

## Third-Party Services

The current app build does not include advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or cloud user accounts.

External apps/services such as YouTube or email clients are governed by their own privacy policies when opened by the user.

## Data Deletion

Users can delete local app data through:

```text
Settings → Clear Data
```

Users can also uninstall the app to remove its local data.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children.
