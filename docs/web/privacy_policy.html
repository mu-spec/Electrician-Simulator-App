Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Summary: VoltMaster Pro is designed as an offline-first reference app. The current app build does not collect, sell, transmit, or share personal data with the developer.

1. Who We Are

VoltMaster Pro is an educational and field-reference application for electricians, apprentices, electrical engineers, solar technicians, and students.

2. Data We Collect

The current version of VoltMaster Pro does not collect personal data on developer-controlled servers.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app may store the following information locally in the app's private storage:

Bookmarks
Saved calculations
Quiz results
Reading history
Language preference
Recent searches
Personal notes
Basic job/project records

This local data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our server by the app.

4. Internet Access

The app may use internet access when you choose to open external links, including YouTube tutorial links, email feedback/support links, and Play Store links. The video tutorial list works offline, but watching YouTube videos requires internet.

5. Third-Party Apps and Services

If you open YouTube, an email app, browser, or Google Play from VoltMaster Pro, those external services are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Permissions

The current app build primarily uses internet access for external links. It does not require location, contacts, SMS, phone, microphone, or account permissions for core offline use.

7. Analytics, Ads, and Accounts

The current closed beta build does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, or user account login. If these features are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before release.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete local data using Settings → Clear Data. You can also remove app data by uninstalling the app.

9. Children's Privacy

VoltMaster Pro does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Safety Disclaimer

VoltMaster Pro provides educational electrical reference content. It is not a substitute for official electrical codes, utility requirements, manufacturer instructions, or qualified professional judgment.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in future app versions. The latest version should be hosted at the public URL provided in the app listing.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: voltmasterpro.app@gmail.com
